Changes made for this adaptation:
Subset of Han ideographs first appearing in the N3 vocabulary, excluding characters already taught in N5/N4 and excluding the iteration mark 々. Entries follow first vocabulary occurrence. The first three English senses are retained when available, whitespace is normalized, Japanese on readings are converted to hiragana, okurigana dots and prefix/suffix hyphens are removed, duplicate readings are removed, and the primary stroke count is used. Dictionary indexes, SKIP codes, and other search codes are not included.

Update procedure (downloads the current official source during development):
  python3 scripts/update-n3-kanji.py
Offline update using a saved source:
  python3 scripts/update-n3-kanji.py --source /path/to/kanjidic2.xml.gz
The generated data are bundled locally. There are no runtime dictionary calls.
