magitower: Arcane Circuit

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Forzen Academy LLC operates the Android and iOS versions of magitower: Arcane Circuit (“Magitower”). This policy explains what information the game and its providers handle, why they use it, and what choices you have. You can play without creating a Magitower account.

Game data on your device

Magitower saves your player name, settings, progress, run records, achievements, unlocks, and in-game inventory on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Your device’s backup or transfer service may copy and restore your save if enabled. The mobile game also keeps a small log of gameplay events on the device and replaces older entries as the log fills. Forzen Academy does not receive that log automatically.

Use Options → Clear Data to reset your game progress and settings on this device. Store purchases are recorded separately and can be restored through the store. Your device or backup provider manages copies held in backups.

Gameplay analytics

Magitower sends gameplay events to Google Analytics so we can understand how people play and improve the game. These events include tutorial progress, runs started, floors reached, upgrades and cosmetics chosen, and interactions with rewarded ads. Analytics data includes a randomly generated identifier, the app platform, and operating system. Google Analytics may also receive device and browser information and estimate your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. Advertising personalization is disabled for this analytics data.

On iPhone and iPad, the game starts sending gameplay events only if you choose to share gameplay stats. To stop future uploads, turn off Share Gameplay Stats in Options. On Android, gameplay analytics uploads are enabled by default. See the Google Privacy Policy for details about how Google handles information.

Advertising and consent

The free mobile game uses Google AdMob to serve ads, including optional rewarded videos. When these services are used, Google and participating ad providers may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from your IP address, app or device identifiers, and information about app launches, ad views, taps, and video interactions. Google may also process performance and diagnostic information such as app launch times or crash data. These providers use information to deliver and measure ads, administer rewards, improve their services, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where permitted. Google may collect the Android advertising ID where available. Google may also collect the Android app set ID for purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention, separate from ad personalization and measurement.

Magitower uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for and remember advertising choices where required. The ad and consent services may store or read information on your device to remember your choices and support ad delivery. When Ad Privacy Options appears in the game’s Options menu, you can review or change your choices there. Ads may still appear based on context when personalization is disabled. You can also use your device’s advertising controls or Google’s ad personalization controls. The Full Version purchase removes ads from the game.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and store reviews

Apple’s App Store or Google Play handles payments and billing details. Magitower uses store product identifiers, transaction or receipt information, and purchase status to verify purchases, unlock content, restore ownership, and handle refunds. The game keeps a local record of verified ownership so purchases can be available when you play offline.

If you submit an in-app rating or review, the store handles it. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice and the Google Privacy Policy for how the stores handle their information.

Multiplayer and sharing

When you host or join a multiplayer room, the game uses PeerJS Cloud and WebRTC to connect devices. Connection and relay services handle IP addresses and connection details to establish or relay the connection. Other players in the room receive your display name, character selections, and gameplay information needed for the shared run. A direct connection may also expose your public IP address to other players in the room.

If you share a game image, Magitower passes it to your device’s share menu. The app or person you choose receives the image and any game information shown in it.

Support, retention, and security

If you contact us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments you send to answer your request and resolve the issue. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the issue, keep necessary records, or meet legal obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Game saves and purchase ownership records stay in the app’s private storage while needed to retain your progress or purchases. Local gameplay logs are limited in size and rotate as new entries are written. Multiplayer connection information is used to set up and maintain the session. Stores, advertising providers, and other service providers manage information they hold under their own retention practices. Ad SDK traffic and multiplayer gameplay traffic use encrypted connections. We require providers that process personal information on our behalf to protect it in line with applicable privacy requirements.

Gameplay analytics is sent over HTTPS. Our Google Analytics retention settings are two months for most event-level data and 14 months for user-level data. New activity associated with the same identifier can restart the user-level retention period. Aggregated reports can be kept longer. You can email us at the address below to request deletion.

Providers and multiplayer participants may be in other countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for transfers of personal information, we apply those safeguards to transfers we make. You can contact us for more information.

Your privacy choices and rights

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we use information as needed to provide the features and purchases you request, to pursue our legitimate interests in supporting the game and preventing abuse, or to meet legal obligations. We ask for consent where required for advertising or related device storage.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain uses, opt out of certain advertising uses, or complain to your local data protection authority. You can withdraw advertising consent through Ad Privacy Options when available. To make a privacy or deletion request, email us. We may ask for enough information to verify and respond to your request. For records held by Apple or Google, you can also use the privacy controls provided by those companies.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, contact us so we can review it and delete it where appropriate.

Contact
