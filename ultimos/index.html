The story

A world built on a beautiful lie.

Black mages rule as humanity’s celebrated protectors. White mages are remembered as ancient tyrants who held civilization beneath them by controlling the healing everyone needed.

When young fugitives Orin and Kisaya break a forbidden seal beneath the city, they awaken the last person who remembers what truly happened. Ultimos is a white mage from the forgotten age—and his magic can do far more than heal.

He may be powerful enough to free the world from tyranny. He may also hate it enough to become something worse.
