How the choice works

Download the branch plan ↓
1

Shared foundation

The base class becomes its first evolution at level 7. Both paths grow from that same character.

2

Choose a specialty

At level 10, choose one of the two named paths. Each offers a different role and element mix.

3

Complete that calling

At level 24, the selected specialist becomes its own final class. Both finals use the same stat budget.

Each fork is a choice of alternative evolutions; no character fusion recipes are defined. The wiki presents the branch design and its artwork. These paths are active in the game. Every base class is available in the starter pool. Every existing adventurer painting is retained, and each new class receives front and rear idle, hurt, and attack poses.
