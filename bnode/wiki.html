Node Catalog

This document describes the nodes currently implemented in magitower: Arcane Circuit. Values and behavior reflect the game code, not earlier design notes.

Every catalog row pairs the exact current in-game copy with a fuller detailed behavior description. The quoted copy is sourced from the same registry used by node cards and inspection modals.

How node effects travel

  • Power is the shared packet value: it becomes damage at Enemies and healing at the Player.
  • Weapons and Player-targeting items are source nodes. They create packets on a timer.
  • Most modifiers are inline nodes. Their effects are applied in the order a packet travels through them.
  • Relics are passive. They do not need wires and work as long as they are on the board.
  • A normal wire traversal takes 0.2 seconds. The delay scales down with that chain's attack speed.
  • Splitter copies carried properties into its branches. Merge preserves distinct contributions but does not duplicate a contribution that came from the same upstream source.
  • Critical chance, critical damage, First Hit, Bleed, Burn chance, Poison, Venom extraction, Flashpoint, Lifesteal, and max-HP damage travel with the packet until it reaches its target. Momentum adds its Power immediately so every downstream node and wire readout sees the empowered packet.
  • Attack speed bonuses from ordinary Attack Speed, Quick Strike, Blitz, Time Warp, and Frenzy nodes are additive within a chain. Fury Potion and Overdrive Serum multiply the resulting speed and also grant global Power. Every dose adds uncapped duration. Overdrive spends its stronger reserve first while ordinary Fury waits underneath, then Fury resumes.

Characters

Sara the Mage

Sara starts each run with a Wand connected to Enemies and her unique Arcane Tome relic directly beneath it. Arcane Tome is character equipment: it is always present for Sara and never appears in the normal reward pool. While playing as Sara, Wand, Fireball, Thunder Sword, Wind Blade, Trident, Pyro Master, and Avatar have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier. Her favored weapons write two Pages per completed attack, while Pyro Master and Avatar support her elemental builds.

Every completed weapon packet writes 1 Page into Arcane Tome. Magical weapons—Wand, Arcane Wand, Fireball, Flare Storm, Phoenix, Thunder Sword, Wind Blade, Tornado, and Trident—write 2 Pages instead. Each Page records that packet's Power. At 6 Pages, the Tome heals Sara for 2 HP and fires an Arcane Missile at the living enemy with the lowest current HP for 75% of the Pages' weighted average Power. The missile uses the packet that completes the Tome's critical chance and critical-damage bonus, but does not inherit statuses, Lifesteal, Ricochet, ammo recovery, or Page generation. An all-enemy attack still writes Pages only once. A full Tome holds its charge between waves when there is no living target, and overflow Pages retain their own Power for the next missile.

Clyde the Thief

Clyde starts with a Dagger connected to Enemies and his unique Skeleton Key relic one cell down and to the left. The first Dagger or Twin Daggers hit against each enemy adds 1 Key pip, and every defeated enemy adds 1 more. At 8 pips, the Key creates one temporary Stolen Good in an open grid cell. Only one Stolen Good can exist at a time, and Key progress pauses until it shatters. If the grid is full, the Key holds at 8 pips and shows Loot Ready until space opens. While playing Clyde, most ammo and item synergies have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier: Crossbow, Potion, Minor Potion, Fury Potion, Healing Cask, Whetstone, Reloader, Recycler, Alchemist, and Broken Memories. Salvager remains available to Clyde, but appears at 75% of its normal rate.

Each Stolen Good is chosen evenly between a Stolen Chest, Powder Bomb, and Smoke Bomb.

  • Stolen Chest: socketless Relic with 2 locks. Each enemy defeated removes one. Opening it awards 3 coins, then it shatters automatically.
  • Powder Bomb: two-ammo weapon that deals 20 Power to every enemy every 6 seconds. It can draw from Reloader or Repeater and shatters automatically after its final attack resolves.
  • Smoke Bomb: socketless Relic with 2 charges. It spends one before an enemy attack, heals 10 HP, and gives that attack a 40% chance to miss. It shatters automatically when empty.

All three Stolen Goods count as ammo-bearing nodes when they shatter, so they can trigger Recycler, Salvager, and Broken Memories. Stolen Chest and Smoke Bomb count as Relics while present, including for Relicmaster. They cannot be replenished by Reloader, Repeater, or Alchemist.

Jam the Pirate

Jam starts with a Pistol connected to Enemies and her unique Jolly Roger relic one cell down and to the left. Pistol and Deadeye packets gain 3% Crit Chance per Bullseye on their target, up to 15%. A landed Pistol-family packet adds 1 of 6 Powder pips and stores its resolved direct damage; a packet that Crits adds a second pip, stores a second copy of its damage, and heals Jam for 2 HP once for the entire packet. Other weapons add 1 pip when they Crit or cash out Bullseye, storing 50% of only their largest resolved hit so multi-target attacks cannot overcharge the relic. At 6 pips, the Jolly Roger deals 10% of all stored damage to every living enemy and gives each survivor 1 Bullseye mark, then resets. A Broadside cannot charge the relic or carry statuses, Lifesteal, Ricochet, or other packet effects. If the final shot of a wave fills the relic, it remains ready until another qualifying packet lands with an enemy still alive.

While playing Jam, Pistol, Katana, Crit Chance, Guarded Edge, Frenzy, Diffraction, and Echo have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier. These rewards provide built-in Crit, improve Crit frequency or payoff, or create additional opportunities to charge Jolly Roger. Jolly Roger continues to work after Pistols fuse into Deadeye.

Pierre the Chemist

Pierre starts with a Plague Flask connected to Enemies and his unique Materia Medica relic one cell down and to the left. A completed weapon activation whose live path carries Poison fills 1 of the relic's 3 Observation pips. Every third qualifying activation is Catalyzed: its weapon Power is multiplied by 1.5 and every Poison contribution it carries or acquires is doubled. Split branches and Echo replays retain the catalyzed formula without adding more pips, while a path stripped by Clean Hit does not qualify.

While playing Pierre, Plague Flask, Toxic Coating, Tainted Strike, Venom Extractor, and Poison Orb have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier. Plague Flask is available to every character and remains supported after fusing into Miasma Bomb.

Finn the Sellsword

Finn starts with an Iron Sword connected to Enemies and his unique Duelist's Tempo relic one cell down and to the left. Every actual weapon activation builds 1 of 5 Tempo pips; activating a different weapon than the previous activation builds 2 pips instead. Target count, Splitter branches, Ricochet, and Echo do not add extra pips, while the individual slashes of a multi-strike weapon count as real activations.

At 5 pips, Finn enters Flourish for 4 seconds and immediately heals 3 HP. Flourish grants 25% global attack speed and 15% Evasion, heals another 3 HP whenever Finn evades an attack, and gives the first attack from each connected weapon 25% more Power. Each weapon can spend this precision bonus once per Flourish. Tempo cannot recharge during Flourish. While playing Finn, weapons and finesse-oriented rewards—Weaponmaster, Attack Speed, Quick Strike, Frenzy, Fury Potion, Cloak and Dagger, Guarded Edge, and Fencer—have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier.

Ramos the Barbarian

Ramos starts with a Greataxe connected to Enemies and his unique Berserker's Heart relic one cell down and to the left. Greataxe deals 30 Power every 4 seconds to a primary random enemy and cleaves up to two other living enemies for 30% Power each. Greataxe is exclusive to Ramos's level-up pool, and three copies fuse into Bloodreaver.

Any direct packet that reaches Enemies with at least 30 pre-critical Power counts as a Mighty Blow and fills 1 of the Heart's 3 Fury pips, once per packet regardless of its target count. Ordinary Mighty Blows add Bleed equal to 10% of impact damage. Every third Mighty Blow becomes Enraged, gaining 40% Power, healing Ramos for 9 HP after dealing damage, and adding Bleed equal to 35% of its impact damage, then resetting the pips. This Bleed is additive with carried Bleed, while Clean Hit suppresses the Bleed but retains the direct Power and healing payoff. While playing Ramos, Heavy Attack, Bleed, Lifesteal, Bloodletter, Overcharger, Capacitor, and Execution have a modest 25% selection-weight bonus within their rolled rarity tier.

Node rarity

Rarity controls how frequently a node is offered, not the strength of a particular copy. A Power +10 node is always Power +10 regardless of when it appears.

  • Common: Iron Sword, Pistol, Greataxe, Dagger, Wind Blade, Plague Flask, Fireball, Wand, Power +10, Quick Strike, Cloak and Dagger, Third Strike, Attack Speed, Crit Chance, Heavy Attack, Execution, Momentum, Bleed, Burn, Tainted Strike, Potion, Minor Potion, and Healing Cask.
  • Uncommon: Katana, Crossbow, Barrier, Guarded Edge, Slayer, Mutagen, Ricochet, Diffraction, Clean Hit, Whetstone, River Force, Fencer, Pyro Master, Frenzy, Giant Slayer, Splitter, Merge, First Hit, Toxic Coating, Venom Extractor, Flashpoint, Overcharger, Reloader, Buckler, Recycler, Healing Orb, Lifesteal, and Fury Potion.
  • Rare: Thunder Sword, Devourer, Midas Touch, Gilded Edge, Avatar, Weaponmaster, Relicmaster, Capacitor, Echo, Overflow, Fire Focus, Exp Jewel, Power Glove, Big Hitter, Glass Cannon, Bloodletter, Poison Orb, Dark Orb, Thorns, Angel's Feather, Alchemist, Broken Memories, Bounty Hunter, and Salvager.
  • Fused: any node created from three matching copies, shown in gold and never offered directly.
  • Ascendant: any fusion created from two fused nodes, shown with a prismatic marker and never offered directly.

Each of the three level-up cards rolls its rarity independently. Levels 2–5 use 70% Common, 25% Uncommon, and 5% Rare odds. Levels 6–12 use 58% Common, 32% Uncommon, and 10% Rare. Level 13 onward uses 48% Common, 36% Uncommon, and 16% Rare. If an eligible tier is empty, its odds are redistributed across the tiers that still have valid rewards.

After four level-up offers without a Rare, the next level-up offer guarantees at least one eligible Rare. Seeing a Rare resets this protection, including when it appears from a reroll. Rerolling an offer without finding a Rare does not advance the protection counter.

Three-copy fusion ingredients gain fusion momentum from loose copies currently on the board. With one loose copy, later copies roll as one tier more common. With two loose copies, the completing copy rolls as two tiers more common, down to Common. Fused results do not count as loose ingredients, so completing a fusion resets that family's momentum.

Run coins

  • Every defeated enemy awards 1 coin.
  • Elite enemies award 3 base coins and bosses award 5 base coins instead.
  • Clearing a wave awards 1 additional coin.
  • Coins are currently run-local and reset when the run restarts; they are not written to saved data yet.
  • Each loose Bounty Hunter makes the single marked enemy take 15% more damage and award 2 bonus coins. Three copies fuse into Death Warrant.
  • Midas Touch adds 10 Power and permanently marks enemies damaged by its chain for 20% more kill coins. Repeated hits from the same Midas Touch do not stack, while distinct copies stack additively. Its multiplier applies to the full enemy reward, including a Bounty Hunter bonus.
  • Gilded Edge is a Clyde-only Rare inline node that converts held wealth into damage without spending it: +10% base damage plus +1% per 2 coins, capped at +50% at 80 coins.
  • Salvager gives ammo-bearing nodes 25% more damage or healing and awards 3 coins whenever the player breaks a depleted one.

Enemy archetypes

Waves mix four enemy roles on a deterministic rotation so their threat is readable and consistent. Steady Skeletons use the baseline damage and attack interval. Swift Bats deal 65% damage but attack in 65% of the time. Heavy Skeletons deal 170% damage but wait 170% as long. Venom Rats deal 56% direct damage and add a Poison pool equal to 22% of baseline attack damage. As that Poison fully decays it contributes about another 44%, keeping each archetype's long-run DPS near the baseline while making Rats meaningfully more Poison-weighted.

Blocking or evading a Venom hit prevents its Poison application. Once applied, hostile Poison ticks once per second, deals damage equal to its current pool, and then halves. It bypasses Barrier and Evasion as a status tick, but ordinary incoming-damage modifiers still apply.

Enemy attack intervals begin shrinking very gently after wave 40: 0.25% per additional wave, capped at a 12.5% reduction. Archetype timing multipliers still apply on top, while attack speed is unchanged through wave 40.

Status effects

Critical hits

Critical chance is rolled when a packet reaches its target. A normal critical hit deals or heals for 2 times the packet value. Critical chance is additive and capped at 100%. Vital Strike can increase the critical multiplier.

Bleed

A Bleed hit stores 50% of its resolved impact damage on the enemy. The next hit ruptures 25% of the current pool as bonus damage and removes that amount from the pool. Bloodletter raises that rupture to 50%. A new Bleed application is added after any existing pool ruptures.

Ramos's Berserker's Heart adds its own Bleed application after a qualifying Mighty Blow lands: 10% of impact damage normally or 35% on every third Enraged blow. This relic Bleed is additive with Bleed already carried by the chain.

Hemorrhage is the fused Bleed modifier. It stores 100% of the hit and makes carried hits rupture 35% of the pool. Bloodletter still takes priority and raises the rupture to 50%.

On a Player-targeting healing chain, Bleed works in reverse: it stores recovery equal to 50% of the resolved direct healing, and a later healing packet releases 25% of the stored recovery as bonus healing. Bloodletter raises this release to 50% too.

Poison

Poison is a stored pool. Once per second, an enemy takes damage equal to the current pool, then the pool is halved. Poison carried by a Player-targeting healing packet is inverted into Poison Recovery: each tick heals the Player by the current pool instead of damaging them, then halves the pool normally. Enemy-applied hostile Poison is tracked separately, so Poison Recovery can heal while hostile Poison damages the Player. Pools clear when their remaining value drops below 0.1. New applications add to the matching pool.

Toxic Coating adds percentage-based Poison, while Tainted Strike and Neurotoxin add flat Poison. If they are in the same chain, all contributions are applied. Poison nodes in a Player-targeting chain create Poison Recovery for the Player. Neurotoxin also weakens an enemy's direct attack damage by 20% for 5 seconds; in a Player-targeting chain it grants the Player 20% multiplicative damage reduction for 5 seconds instead.

Venom Extractor adds 5 flat Poison, applies every other Poison contribution from the current hit, then consumes 25% of the target's resulting Poison pool and deals three times the consumed amount immediately as Poison damage. Multiple Extractors and Echo replays add both another 5 Poison and another 25% extraction, up to consuming the full pool.

Plague Coating adds Poison equal to 70% of the hit. When a poisoned enemy dies, 50% of the Poison remaining on it moves to the next living enemy in the wave.

Burn

Burn deals 20% of the triggering impact damage once per second for three seconds. Burn normally does not stack. Applying Burn again refreshes its duration without delaying the next tick and keeps whichever Burn DPS value is higher. Fire Focus allows up to three independent Burns; at the limit, an equal or stronger new Burn replaces the weakest one.

Flashpoint adds 5 Power, then consumes every remaining tick on the target and deals their combined damage immediately at 1.5×. A Burn applied by the same hit is included before Flashpoint detonates it.

Inferno is guaranteed Burn at 30% of the triggering hit per second for four seconds. The normal strongest-Burn replacement rule still applies.

Wind slow

Wind slow makes the affected enemy's attack timer advance 25% more slowly for four seconds. Another slowing hit refreshes the duration.

Barrier

Each Barrier blocks one complete enemy attack, then is consumed. The Player can store a maximum of 3 Barriers; excess grants are discarded. Barriers are spent one at a time and persist between waves. Poison damage is a status tick rather than an enemy attack, so it bypasses Barrier.

While a connected Aegis is active, consuming any Barrier grants Aegis Guard for 1.25 seconds. Aegis Guard reduces incoming damage by 20%, stacks multiplicatively with effects such as Buckler, and refreshes rather than stacking when triggered again.

Evasion

While Evasion is active, each enemy attack has an independent chance to miss completely. An evaded attack deals no damage, applies no effects, and does not consume a Barrier. Status ticks such as Poison are not enemy attacks and cannot be evaded. Distinct active Evasion nodes stack multiplicatively against the enemy's remaining hit chance, capped at 75% total Evasion; for example, two 10% Cloaks combine to 19%, while a 10% Cloak and a 30% Nightblade combine to 37%. Every node tracks and refreshes its own duration, so another pass through one node does not extend the others. Cloak and Dagger grants 10% Evasion for four seconds. Nightblade grants 30% Evasion for five seconds and banks a Riposte stack whenever an attack is evaded during its own active window.

Bullseye

Pistol hits add one Bullseye mark to that individual enemy, up to five. Deadeye hits add two and prime their marks for Perfect Shot. The next direct packet containing a weapon other than Pistol or Deadeye consumes every mark before crit and status calculations, multiplying that packet's Power by 6% per mark (up to 30%). Consuming all five while the pool is Deadeye-primed becomes a Perfect Shot for +45% Power instead. Pistol- and Deadeye-only packets, Poison/Burn ticks, Thorns, Dark Orb, and other non-weapon damage do not consume marks. Marks and their Perfect Shot priming disappear when their enemy is defeated.

Super fusions combine two already-fused nodes. The earlier-placed ingredient becomes the result, keeps its grid position, and intelligently preserves as much valid external wiring as the new node's sockets allow.
